# Third-party components

- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**: bundled WOFF2 files in `assets/`; the full SIL Open Font License and copyright notice are in `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Ultralytics / YOLO**: installed separately. Model weights are not bundled or committed. See the installed distribution's license and [Ultralytics licensing](https://www.ultralytics.com/license) before redistributing code or weights.
- **Streamlit, OpenCV, NumPy, PySerial, lap and transitive dependencies**: installed through pip and retain their respective package licenses. These libraries are not vendored into the source release.
- **ESP32 Arduino core**: installed separately through Arduino Boards Manager; not bundled.

No right to redistribute user camera recordings, phone photos or annotated toy-vehicle samples is granted by this source package; those files remain local.
