RoadGuardian AI™
 An intelligent digital platform developed to introduce a new model for community-based road safety, integrating artificial intelligence (AI), computer vision, digital twin technology, predictive analytics, interactive awareness, and environmental sustainability into one unified ecosystem.

The concept is based on shifting from the traditional reactive approach to traffic accidents and hazards toward proactive risk prediction, preventive intervention, and measurable impact assessment on human safety, behavior, and the surrounding environment.

The platform analyzes vehicle and pedestrian movements, predicts potential conflict zones and high-risk areas, tests intervention scenarios using digital twin technology, and provides intelligent recommendations to reduce risks and enhance road safety.

It also features a Community Awareness AI module, which transforms real-world traffic situations into interactive educational and practical experiences. These experiences help students, families, and pedestrians develop preventive safety behaviors while measuring improvements in knowledge and awareness before and after the initiative.

The platform further integrates an Environmental Intelligence & Sustainability Digital Twin module (Ecotwin), which evaluates the expected environmental impact of traffic-related decisions by estimating fuel consumption and emissions and comparing alternative scenarios.

A key distinguishing feature of the initiative is the availability of a ready-to-use AI model and an already-developed digital platform, along with the integration of simulation scenarios into an interactive Excel dashboard that displays the results of proposed changes and tracks key performance indicators (KPIs).
